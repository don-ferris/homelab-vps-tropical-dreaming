<p>We left at 6:15 AM for our 9 AM appointment. What should have been a 90 minute drive took just slightly longer…</p>

<p>We passed our destination at  7:55 and continued on toward PriceSmart (Panama’s Costco) because there’s no parking at immigration.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, we passed PriceSmart inadvertently and a series of u-turns cost us 15 minutes. When we finally got there, a bicycle Security Guard at PriceSmart wouldn’t let us park and walk out of the lot  — even though PriceSmart isn’t open yet and we actually plan to do some shopping there when we’re done here. We had to go park at a nearby bank that has paid parking. Another 15 minutes lost. </p>
<p>We started walking toward the immigration office, maybe a half mile away, but Krista’s having a bad pain day so we flagged down a cab (first cab ride in Panama!) and  FINALLY made it to the immigration office with only 10 minutes to spare.</p>
