<p>This will be a long post, and not a happy one. (Frankly, it's difficult just to write this.) We probably needed twice as much time to get all of this done. Realistically, though, it  probably would have ended up just like this no matter when we had started because for me, this was profoundly overwhelming (I was like a deer in the headlights in the face of the chaos that was our house in Pahrump) and, for Krista, the house was her last connection to her parents; she was deeply conflicted about saying goodbye to this place. As a result, we fell into old habits and just... avoided.</p>
