<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal; min-height: 13.8px;"></p>
<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal;">1. Beer/Coke/etc. is priced (shelf tag) and sold INDIVIDUALLY. It may be in a 6-pack or 12-pack on the shelf but the only place it’s sold that way is PriceSmart (Panama’s Costco). When I really want to splurge, a bottle of Little Sump’n Sump’n costs me a little less than $3. Dead Guy Ale is $3.50. Crappy Panamanian lagers are less but I can’t really quote prices because I don’t pay that much attention.</p>
<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal;">2. There are A FEW cops with radar guns (mostly they just use cameras) but there are NO traffic cops — and everyone drives accordingly. NOBODY stops at stop signs. Most people don’t even slow down.</p>
<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal;">3. A red light means “go” here. A green light means “go”, too — but be careful because the other guy has a red! (That’s a joke. People actually DO observe red and green lights here.)</p>
<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal;">4. Paper towels and toilet paper are super THIN here. The expensive/premium stuff is a little thicker but nothing like Charmin or Bounty, etc. </p>
<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal;">5. There are NO street addresses. Literally - not exaggerating. Forget about plugging an address into Google Maps or Waze - you can find most places by entering the store/company name into GV/Waze (but you need an approximate idea of where it’s located so you can kinda sanity check the results) - but the app is going to guide you there via latitude/longitude coordinates.</p>
<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal;">6. Per above, there’s no postal service (at least not as you know it) — nobody gets mail delivered to their house (not daily, not ever). “Junk mail” does not exist here. If you want to send mail to someone in another town in Panama, you write (on the envelope/package) their name, their phone number, and the town they live in. Then you take it to a “Correo” (a store - found in most malls - that’s a lot like a post office), pay the “postage” (more than USPS, less than Fedex), and when it gets to the Correo in/near that town, the Correo will call the phone number on the envelope/package and say, “Come get your mail.”</p>
<p style="margin: 0px; font-style: normal; font-variant-caps: normal; font-stretch: normal; font-size: 12px; line-height: normal; font-family: Helvetica; font-size-adjust: none; font-kerning: auto; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; font-variant-position: normal; font-variant-emoji: normal; font-feature-settings: normal; font-optical-sizing: auto; font-variation-settings: normal;">7. Per above, whether you use Google Maps or Waze, the street names they give you do not match up with the street signs (on those extremely rare occasions when there IS a street sign) AT ALL. You just have to watch the colored line on your screen AS WELL AS the gray lines that show you the forks and intersections where you COULD but SHOULDN’T turn. What they have INSTEAD of street signs are signs with destinations. (As in “turn here to go to…”) But those names almost NEVER align with the names that GV/Maps uses.</p>
