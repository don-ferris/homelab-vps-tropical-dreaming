<li>There are A FEW cops with radar guns (mostly they just use cameras) but there are NO traffic cops — and everyone drives accordingly. NOBODY stops at stop signs. Most people don’t even slow down. A red light means “go” here. A green light means “go”, too — but be careful because the other guy has a red! (That’s a joke. People actually DO observe red and green lights here.)</li>
<li>Paper towels and toilet paper are super THIN here. The expensive/premium stuff is a little thicker but nothing like Charmin or Bounty, etc.</li>
<li>At malls, grocery stores, wherever, Panamanians <em>back into</em> parking spots. </li>
